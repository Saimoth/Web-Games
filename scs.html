Do you only work on Italian cars?

No. Fiat, Abarth and Alfa Romeo are our specialist marques, but we also work on other makes and models. Call with your vehicle details to discuss what you need.

My car has an awkward fault. Can I talk it through?

Yes. Diagnostics are a core part of our work, and other garages turn to us for technical problems. Tell us the symptoms and your vehicle details so we can discuss an assessment.

How do I arrange a visit?

Call 01489 861112 or 07539 766538 to speak to the workshop. You can also use “Prepare a text enquiry” to draft a message on your device. A visit or booking needs confirmation from the workshop.
